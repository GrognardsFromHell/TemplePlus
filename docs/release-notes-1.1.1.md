# Temple+ 1.1.1

A hotfix for 1.1.0.

## Bug fixes
- Fixes summoned creatures disappearing when saving the game. A familiar or other summon could fade out and leave the party a few seconds after a save (reported by a Co8 player after updating to 1.1.0). Thanks to Codex for the fix: [#957](https://github.com/GrognardsFromHell/TemplePlus/pull/957).
